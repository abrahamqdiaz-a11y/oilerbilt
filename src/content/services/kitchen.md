## Start with how the kitchen fails you

Most homeowners do not need a vague promise of a “dream kitchen.” They need more prep space, storage that is reachable, drawers that work, better lighting, a finish that can be cleaned, or a layout that stops people from colliding at dinner. OilerBilt plans Houston kitchen remodeling around those daily problems.

Some kitchens need a full removal. Others improve substantially by keeping the plumbing locations, replacing cabinets and surfaces, correcting damaged walls and updating paint and tile. Keeping a sound layout can reduce trade work and uncertainty. Moving everything can make sense when circulation is genuinely broken. The estimate should explain which changes are doing useful work.

## Kitchens in southwest Houston housing stock

Alief, Mission Bend and nearby communities include many ranch, split-level and two-story homes from the 1970s through the 1990s. Common kitchen issues include soffits that make rooms feel low, shallow pantry storage, worn builder cabinets, busy tile counters and closed-off breakfast areas. Not every soffit is empty, and not every wall can disappear. Investigation comes before a demolition promise.

When cabinets come out, older leak damage or patched drywall may appear. Slab movement can also show up in uneven floors and cracked tile. These existing conditions affect installation and should be handled openly before new finishes cover them.

## Humidity, water and material choices

Houston humidity and plumbing leaks are hard on particleboard cabinet boxes, swollen trim and poorly sealed sink bases. Ventilation, water-resistant finishes and careful sealing at wet areas matter more than showroom language. Material choices should also account for cleaning, direct sun and how the household actually cooks.

## Living through the work

A kitchen project affects meals, storage and the main path through many homes. Before starting, identify what stays accessible, where appliances will go, and when water or power may be interrupted. Remove fragile items from nearby rooms and plan for dust even with containment. Clear expectations make the temporary inconvenience manageable.

## Permitting in Houston and Harris County

Cabinet, countertop and paint work may not trigger the same requirements as moving circuits, gas, plumbing or structural openings. City of Houston rules differ from work in unincorporated Harris County. We review the project address and intended changes before stating what approvals apply.
