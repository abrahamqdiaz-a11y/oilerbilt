## Paint cannot hide bad preparation

Fresh paint makes a fast visual difference, but it also highlights dents, flashing patches, loose caulk and uneven texture. OilerBilt provides interior and exterior painting in Houston with the repair and preparation defined before finish coats begin.

Interior work may include walls, ceilings, trim, doors and coordinated drywall patches. Exterior work starts with the material and exposure: siding, trim, masonry and previously coated surfaces do not all need the same preparation. Commercial jobs add operating hours, customer access and protection requirements to the plan.

## Gulf Coast exposure

Heat, direct sun, wind-driven rain and humidity are hard on exterior coatings. A product chosen only by color can fail early if the surface is dirty, damp, chalky or incompatible. Weather windows matter during application, and joints that allow water in should be addressed rather than painted over.

Inside, humidity and air-conditioning cycles can reveal weak drywall joints or bathroom ceiling problems. Kitchens and bathrooms need washable, appropriate coatings, but higher sheen is not a repair for moisture that has no path out.

## Occupied homes and businesses

Painting becomes easier when the sequence is clear. We discuss furniture, wall hangings, pets, room access and drying time. In commercial spaces, work may need to be phased around customers or staff. That planning belongs in the scope, not in a last-minute conversation after everything is covered.

## Color, sheen and honest expectations

Dark-to-light changes, intense colors and repaired areas may need additional coats or a dedicated primer. Touching up an older wall can flash because the original paint has aged. In many cases, painting the full wall from corner to corner gives the cleaner result.

Preparation can uncover soft drywall, active leaks or deteriorated exterior material. Painting those conditions would make the finish temporary. We identify what is visible and discuss repair before coating it.
