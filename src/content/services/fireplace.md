## A focal wall should fit the room, not fight it

A custom fireplace wall can organize a living room that currently feels unfinished: one deliberate center, space for a television or art, and storage that looks planned instead of added later. OilerBilt builds fireplace and accent walls in Houston with the framing, drywall, finish surfaces, trim and paint considered as one job.

The existing fireplace sets the rules. Clearances, vents, controls and manufacturer requirements are checked before a wall is framed or a television position is promised. A good-looking elevation is not useful if it blocks service access or puts a finish where heat makes it a bad choice.

## Planning the layout

We start with wall width, ceiling height, traffic through the room and what the wall has to hold. Built-ins may need to hide equipment without trapping heat. Shelves need useful depth. A mantel should relate to the firebox and the room rather than land at an arbitrary line.

Houston houses often have large open living spaces, angled corners or older brick surrounds that dominate the room. Some projects call for selective removal and a cleaner surface. Others work better by keeping sound material and building a balanced composition around it. The site review determines which path avoids unnecessary demolition.

## Finish choices in Gulf Coast conditions

Interior humidity and air-conditioning cycles can expose weak joints and poorly prepared surfaces. Drywall transitions, panel seams and trim joints need the right backing and preparation. Material samples also look different under Houston daylight than they do under store lighting, so color and sheen should be judged in the room.

If electrical or low-voltage work is needed, it should be coordinated before the wall closes. Access for future equipment changes matters too. We identify those decisions early because opening a finished feature wall to move one cable is an avoidable problem.

## Permits and existing conditions

Decorative finish work is not the same as modifying a gas line, electrical circuit or structural opening. Project requirements vary by scope and jurisdiction, including inside Houston city limits versus unincorporated Harris County. We do not make a blanket permit claim before the wall and requested work are known.
