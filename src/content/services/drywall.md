## Drywall damage is usually telling you something

A soft ceiling, a brown ring, a joint that keeps opening, or cracks that run from a door corner are not all the same repair. Houston homes see roof leaks, air-conditioning drain problems, burst pipes after freezes, storm-driven water and movement from slab settlement. Before closing the wall, the first job is understanding why it failed.

OilerBilt handles drywall installation and repair across Houston and southwest Harris County. That includes small impact holes, ceiling patches, larger cutouts after plumbing access, water-damaged sections, texture work and complete board installation as part of a remodel. The goal is a sound repair that is ready for paint, not a quick smear over a problem that is still active.

### Water and storm damage

Drywall that has absorbed water can lose strength, sag and trap moisture against insulation or framing. The leak must be stopped first. Then the affected area can be opened as needed, damaged material removed, and the cavity allowed to dry. Covering damp material is faster on day one and expensive later.

Houston’s hurricane season creates sudden demand for interior repairs. We recommend documenting damage before demolition when an insurance claim may be involved. OilerBilt does not promise claim coverage or act as an adjuster; the work scope should match the actual condition found after safe access is available.

### Freeze-damage interior rebuilds

When a pipe bursts, the plumbing repair is only the first half of the job. Wet ceilings and walls may need removal, drying, new drywall, tape, finish, texture and paint. Access holes made by plumbers can also require careful backing and blending. We can review the interior rebuild once the line is repaired and the area is dry.

### Popcorn ceiling removal in Houston

Popcorn texture is common in Houston-area houses built from the 1960s through the 1980s. Removal is more than scraping. Painted texture can be difficult to release, old repairs may show once the surface is bare, and the ceiling normally needs skim work, sanding, primer and paint. Unknown older materials should be handled honestly; testing may be appropriate before disturbance.

### Cracks and slab movement

Drywall cracks can come from normal movement, poor previous finishing or foundation movement. A cosmetic patch cannot stabilize a slab. When a crack is active, unusually wide, paired with sticking doors or accompanied by exterior masonry movement, a homeowner may need the underlying condition evaluated before finish work. Once movement is addressed, the repair can be opened, reinforced where appropriate, finished and blended.

### City permits and unincorporated Harris County

Simple finish repair usually differs from work that changes plumbing, electrical systems or structural framing. Requirements also differ between the City of Houston and unincorporated Harris County. We confirm the project address and scope before saying whether a permit is involved; broad internet answers are not a substitute for the authority with jurisdiction.
