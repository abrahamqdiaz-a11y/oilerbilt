## Commercial work starts with operations

A business remodel is not only a construction plan. It is also an access plan: when noise is acceptable, which doors stay clear, where material can be staged and how customers or staff move around the work. OilerBilt handles focused commercial remodeling, drywall and painting across the Houston area with those constraints discussed up front.

The confirmed service scope includes residential and commercial general contracting, painting, drywall and interior finish work. We do not list specialized building services that have not been confirmed. The first review determines whether the requested project fits OilerBilt’s scope and what trade or permit coordination may be involved.

## Occupied spaces and phasing

An occupied office, shop or service space needs separation between work and daily activity. Dust control, wet-paint areas, equipment access and debris routes should be identified before the start date. Phasing can reduce closure time, but it may also extend the calendar. The estimate should make that tradeoff visible.

## Existing conditions

Commercial interiors often contain previous tenant work, patched walls and concealed routing that does not match the visible finish. Removal can reveal damage or code-related questions that could not be confirmed from the surface. We document the condition and discuss any change before covering it.

## Houston jurisdiction and approvals

City of Houston requirements are not identical to those in neighboring municipalities or unincorporated Harris County. Work that changes walls, plumbing, electrical systems or occupancy conditions may require plans or inspections. The project address, use and scope have to be known before giving a responsible answer.

## Closeout

Finish work is reviewed with a punch list rather than assumed complete from across the room. Touchups, hardware, wall surfaces and the agreed work areas are checked together so the handoff has a clear endpoint.
