## Drywall after leaks and freezes

Winter pipe failures can leave access holes and wet wall cavities long after the plumbing is restored. Roof and air-conditioning drain leaks can do the same. The repair begins after the source is stopped. Damaged material is removed as needed, the cavity is allowed to dry, and new drywall is supported, finished and prepared for paint.

Texture matching is a blend, not a stamped copy. Existing texture, accumulated paint and changing daylight affect the result. Full-wall or full-ceiling painting may be the better finish when an older surface cannot be touched up invisibly.

## Kitchens, bathrooms and living spaces

Kitchen remodeling should solve storage and circulation before it adds decorative detail. Bathroom remodeling should solve waterproofing and ventilation before the tile pattern gets attention. Fireplace walls should respect heat clearances and equipment access before framing closes. Those priorities keep the visible work connected to how the room performs.

For occupied homes, the sequence matters. Families need to know when water, power, cooking space or bathroom access will be interrupted. Dust containment helps, but remodeling still creates noise and traffic. We discuss those realities before scheduling.

## Exterior paint and Gulf Coast exposure

West-facing surfaces around Katy take strong sun, and all exteriors face humidity and heavy rain. Loose coating, open joints and moisture entry need attention before paint. A premium product cannot compensate for a wet, chalky or poorly prepared surface.
