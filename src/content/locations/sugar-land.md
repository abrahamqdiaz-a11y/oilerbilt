## Kitchens, baths and family-room focal walls

Kitchen decisions start with circulation and storage. Keeping plumbing and major appliance locations can control disruption, while targeted cabinet and surface changes improve daily use. When a wall or soffit is proposed for removal, investigation is necessary because ducts, wiring or framing may occupy the space.

Bathroom projects need the same discipline behind the finish. Tile is the visible layer; substrate, waterproofing, ventilation and clean transitions determine whether it holds up. Houston-area humidity makes effective exhaust and appropriate coatings particularly important.

Custom fireplace walls are a distinct option for Sugar Land family rooms with tall ceilings or large open plans. Built-ins, television placement, mantel proportions and equipment access should be coordinated before framing. Existing fireplace clearances remain the controlling requirement.

## Storms, leaks and drywall repair

Roof leaks, air-conditioning drain issues and freeze-related pipe failures can leave localized or extensive interior damage. After the water source is fixed, wet material may need removal and the cavity needs time to dry. Texture and paint can then be blended with honest expectations about older finishes.

Settlement cracks also deserve context. A drywall repair can restore a stable surface, but it does not correct foundation movement. Repeated cracks or related door and floor symptoms may need evaluation before cosmetic work.
